Our Story

Where Passion Meets Flavor

Founded in 2010 by Chef Marco Rossi, Savory & Co. began as a small neighborhood bistro with a big dream: to create a dining experience that feels both luxurious and welcoming.

Today, our kitchen is led by a team of award-winning chefs who share a deep commitment to sustainability, seasonality, and the art of hospitality. Every dish tells a story — from the local farms we partner with to the craft cocktails shaken at our bar.
